The world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026 on one 1024×1024 front page: 48 stories from 7 regions and 7 fields, 15 of them illustrated. The leads are the collapse of the COP31 climate talks in Antalya, the Russian drone strike on a block of flats in Dymer near Kyiv, and the fire at a sweets factory in Banten, Indonesia. A bar counts stories by region and field, a strip gives the day in numbers, a map pins every story, an "Also on" list carries 33 short briefs, and a green box names the stories left out because they were first reported on 21 November.

Stories come from about 26 news feeds (BBC, Guardian, NYT, Al Jazeera, France 24, Anadolu, CNA, Straits Times, ABC, RNZ and others), cross-checked against a research pass by Perplexity Computer running Gemini 4 Neon. The illustrations were made by Perplexity Computer as flat pictograms; every word on the page is typeset, not generated.

## Run it

```sh
python main.py   # copies entry.png to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (or /output)
```
